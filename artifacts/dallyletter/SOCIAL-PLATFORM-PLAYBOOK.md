## TikTok — short educational demonstrations
**Bio:** African learning innovation 🌍 | Zimbabwe first 🇿🇼 | South Africa and Africa welcome | Built by Elisha Maposa.

**Video structure (20–40 seconds):**
- 0–3 sec: one specific learner problem or question.
- 3–25 sec: explain one practical idea or demonstrate one verified feature.
- 25–35 sec: show the takeaway and one next action.
- End: Dallyletter Elidems name and official website.

**Starter scripts:**
1. “You reread a page three times. Can you explain it without looking? Try active recall: close the notes, say the main idea, then check what you missed.”
2. “Assignments feel big when you see the whole task. Break it into: understand the instruction, list the deliverable, complete one step, then check before submitting.”
3. “Here is one real Dallyletter workflow.” Record only a feature that is live and publicly accessible; narrate what it does without showing learner names, messages or private records.

Use captions, clear audio and readable text. Do not use fake testimonials, engagement bait or copyrighted music without the required rights.

## YouTube — searchable explainers and product walkthroughs
**Channel description:** Dallyletter Elidems is building digital learning experiences for learners, educators and schools. Zimbabwe is our initial focus, with South Africa as an important early market, with a wider ambition to support diverse African educational contexts and welcome global participation. Founded and solely created by Elisha Maposa.

**Channel trailer outline (45–60 seconds):**
1. Introduce Dallyletter Elidems and its education purpose.
2. Explain Zimbabwe and South Africa as the initial focus and the international outlook.
3. Show only verified, current product screens.
4. Explain who the platform is for: learners, educators and schools.
5. Invite viewers to explore the official website and follow future practical learning guides.

**Searchable video series:**
- Study smarter: active recall and spaced practice.
- Learner walkthrough: finding lessons and completing an assignment (record after verifying the live flow).
- Educator walkthrough: preparing a lesson and supporting learners (record only verified workflows).
- Building Dallyletter: transparent founder development updates.
- Curriculum conversations: explain only the specific syllabus/topic actually covered; invite corrections and requests.

Each video needs a plain-language title, useful description, chapters for longer videos, accurate captions and a thumbnail with one clear idea. Avoid keyword stuffing.

## Measurement
Record a baseline on launch day, then review weekly:
- Reach and impressions by platform.
- Video views and average watch time.
- Saves, shares, meaningful comments and profile visits.
- Website sessions referred by each platform.
- Registrations and learner activation from social referrals.
- Educator enquiries and institutional conversations.

Use platform analytics and tagged campaign links only after official accounts exist. Do not invent baselines, follower counts or engagement results.

## Account security and publishing gate
- Check handle availability; secure matching names where possible.
- Enable two-factor authentication and store recovery codes securely.
- Add the canonical website after confirming it is publicly reachable without login or deployment protection.
- Use only approved brand artwork and permitted product screenshots.
- Keep all account credentials private; never commit tokens or recovery codes to the repository.
- Do not announce a profile until the account exists and the founder approves the launch.
