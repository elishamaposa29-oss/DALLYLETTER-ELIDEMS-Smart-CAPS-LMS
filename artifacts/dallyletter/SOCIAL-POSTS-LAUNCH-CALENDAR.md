## Publishing rules
- Publish only after the official website is confirmed publicly accessible.
- Use screenshots of the current live product, not mockups presented as completed features.
- Never claim accreditation, national recognition, partnerships, learner counts, pass rates or full continental syllabus coverage without evidence.
- Get permission before publishing identifiable learners, teachers or school logos.
- Do not publish account credentials, personal phone numbers, private emails or learner records.
- Set up 2FA, recovery email and recovery codes before opening public accounts.
- Reserve consistent handles only after checking availability on each platform.
- Reply to questions honestly; record syllabus requests and feature feedback for the roadmap.
